Title droughts

The shaded bands on the chart are the first group: runs of five seasons or more in which McLaren won neither championship. Single-championship gaps are listed after them only where they differ — which is to say, only where the other title interrupted them: Hunt in 1976, Häkkinen in 1999 and Hamilton in 2008 each won a drivers' title inside a constructors' drought, and Hamilton's win is what splits the 25-year one into the two shorter bands above.

Bands mark gaps between titles of either kind, so a title win can never sit inside one; banding on the constructors' championship alone would have put Hamilton's 2008 title in the middle of a shaded 25-year run. In 2007 McLaren was excluded from the Constructors' Championship over the espionage case and finished the season on zero points — shown above as a break in the constructors' line. Its drivers kept their places: Lewis Hamilton and Fernando Alonso ended the year tied on points, one behind the champion. Driver positions are full-season championship finishes, so a driver who split a year between teams — Peter Gethin in 1971, who scored most of his points after moving to BRM — carries points earned elsewhere.

Drivers' champions

Niki Lauda is the only driver to have won a title in both a Ferrari and a McLaren — 1975 and 1977 in red, either side of the Nürburgring crash that nearly killed him, then 1984 in papaya. He is on both lists and no one else is: see the McLaren chart. Lewis Hamilton, McLaren's 2008 champion, has driven for Ferrari since 2025.

Title droughts

The shaded bands on the chart are the first group: runs of five seasons or more in which Ferrari won neither championship. Single-championship gaps are listed after them only where they differ — and no constructors' gap does, because every one of Ferrari's has been total. That is not true of every team: McLaren's 25-year constructors' drought was broken mid-way by Hamilton's 2008 drivers' title.

The Constructors' Championship (WCC) didn't exist before 1958, so 1950–1957 shows only the Drivers' line. In 1997, Michael Schumacher was disqualified from that year's Drivers' Championship after the collision with Jacques Villeneuve at the European GP — shown above as "DSQ" — leaving Eddie Irvine as the team's official best-placed finisher (7th).
